Tester tasks

  1. Scan the QR code. Without help, choose a dish and open Customize & View.
  2. Remove an included ingredient. Add a paid extra on pizza or chicken. Explain the displayed price. Removing included ingredients should not discount it.
  3. Try Separate, Stack and Reset. Check that selections and the demo price reset together.
  4. Open View on My Table, allow camera access if asked, and place the dish on a well-lit table. Walk around it.
  5. Change ingredients in camera AR. Stack again and inspect for gaps, incorrect colors or unexpected movement.
  6. Leave AR using the phone’s Close or Back control. Return to the menu and reopen the dish.
  7. Record what was confusing or visually inaccurate. Don’t assume the model proves portion size, ingredients, nutrition or allergen safety.

Physical-device checklist

Run separately on iPhone Safari and an AR-compatible Android Chrome phone, for all three dishes. Record model, OS and browser versions.

  • Camera launches; denial gives useful recovery and 3D stays usable.
  • Placement, tracking, scale and lighting are acceptable.
  • Every offered ingredient changes in the camera view; multiple selections and rapid changes remain consistent.
  • Separate/Stack works; removed ingredients leave no reserved stack gaps.
  • Chicken and buns remain fixed; pizza crust and sauce remain fixed.
  • Leaving/re-entering AR, rotating the phone and switching dishes do not lose page selections or leak selections into another dish or restaurant.
  • Android live controls and displayed price match. iPhone’s native AR has no live price and does not return its changes to the page: match changes on the page after exiting. The page must say this clearly.
  • Test unsupported AR, slow network, unavailable dish, and direct-link refresh.

Testing status: automated checks and desktop previews were performed. No physical-device pilot results are claimed. Prior user reports confirm burger camera ingredient controls; the redesigned flow still needs this device checklist.
