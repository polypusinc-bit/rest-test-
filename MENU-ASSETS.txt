The Test Kitchen — prototype assets
Pizza and chicken sandwich: original procedural prototype geometry created for this project. Editable Blender sources are included in the local models-source directory. These are illustrations, not scans or accurate representations of food for sale.
Burger: Grab3D CC0 model, https://grab3d.com/models/food/burger/ . See MODEL-LICENSE.txt.
Menu images are renders of these models.
Viewer library: Google model-viewer 4.1.0. See MODEL-VIEWER-LICENSE.txt.
