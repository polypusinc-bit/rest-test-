    Tap “View on your table”

    Allow camera access if asked. On iPhone, choose AR if the viewer opens in Object mode. Open the configuration menu to choose ingredients and Arrangement.

    Move your phone slowly over a well-lit table. Follow the placement prompt, then walk around your burger.

AR didn’t open?

Open this page in Safari or Chrome instead of a social app’s browser. Android requires Chrome with WebXR and Google Play Services for AR on a supported device. Update iOS if the iPhone configuration menu is unavailable. If camera placement is unavailable, you can still rotate the burger in 3D.

iPhone fallback: Open the iPhone AR file.
